TinyFix Hotkey

Select English text in any Windows app, then press your shortcut
(default: Ctrl+Alt+Space) to correct spelling and real-word mix-ups
such as form/from, their/there, and to/too.

The correction model is paperweight/tinyfix by Chris Ahlgren
(https://huggingface.co/paperweight/tinyfix). It is bundled with this
installer and runs fully offline after setup. TinyFix Hotkey is not
affiliated with the model author.

Limitations
- English only. Other languages, including Persian, are left unchanged.
- The shortcut will not reach windows that are running as Administrator
  unless TinyFix itself is also elevated.
- The tool still misses some errors; it would rather leave a word alone
  than change one you meant.

Privacy
After installation, TinyFix works entirely on this computer. It does not
send your text or clipboard to a server.
